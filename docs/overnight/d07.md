## D07-A local candidate

`boss.ts` provides serializable definitions/state, weighted selection from phase-specific attack pools with no immediate repeats, health or completed-attack phase triggers, one-time transition/death/completion events, and freely escapable circular arenas. Existing `createRng` advances the combat seed. `bossHooks` composes around existing `skillHooks`; the same `stepCombat`, `stepController` and frame timeline own windup/active/recovery, navigation, stagger, reaction guard, hitstun, resistances, hit/death and XP resolution. No second damage or combat engine exists. Shapes lock origin/direction at windup and hit once at active entry through `applyCombatHit`, checking shape and navigation LOS again. No target tracking or post-interrupt strike.

Attack timings are authored positive integer frames; warnings require at least18 frames. Balance must additionally provide enough distance/time to escape each authored shape. Phase changes wait for idle, stop movement and grant configured preparation time. Leaving the arena or player death resets living boss health/phase/commitment without a completion event; no walls trap the player. Dead bosses cannot reset. Surviving encounters are transient here; durable world completion/rewards belong to D07-B.
