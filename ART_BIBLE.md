# Art bible — proposal, approval pending

Original ashbound gothic frontier: worn basalt, pale bone, tarnished bronze, charcoal fabric; amber fire contrasts cool desaturated stone. No copied characters or environments. 2:1 isometric diamond at 64×32 logical pixels; humanoid baseline 32×64 with foot pivot. Eight directions at consistent scale, silhouette approved before animation. Minimum planned actions: idle, walk, run, attack, heavy attack, cast, hit, block, death. Equipment must preserve silhouette and anchor consistency.

Diffuse/baked shadow foundation with selective dynamic lights and normals. Warm hazards and cold ambient light; avoid burying enemies in darkness. Ground decals below actors; sort actors by foot-world depth; tall occluders require future transparency rules. UI uses readable text, restrained stone/bronze motifs and non-color rarity markers. Effects show windup/active/recovery and respect reduced motion/flash settings. Atlas size/memory and download budgets must precede production.

Current scene is procedural placeholder geometry and 50 sentinel markers only. It is an engine test, not approved visual direction. No mass asset production until G1 approval. Export pipeline and eight-direction animation validation are planned DBL-023–025.

## G1 reference kit — The Veiled Cloister

One abandoned rain-dark cloister is the visual prototype: detailed damp flagstones, ivy-clad gothic stonework, warm bronze lanterns and cool ambient haze. The cloaked wanderer has an ash-grey silhouette and bronze clasp, with eight isometric directions and idle/walk only. Generated source art is retained, reviewed and normalized into anchored atlases. Production animation beyond locomotion and equipment appearance are deferred. The walkable central clearing is authored separately from the painted background; tall movable props use foot-depth sorting. This small reference kit is submitted at G1, not approval for mass asset production.

Generated sources can deviate from exact requested pixel grids. Exporters normalize per-frame alpha bounds, dimensions and foot pivots; validation rejects malformed or empty frames. Background lighting/shadows are baked, with selective runtime lantern glow, player/prop lighting, contact shadows and drifting mist. The illustrated backdrop is a fixed camera plate with authored traversal bounds; it is not a procedural tile renderer or general multi-height architecture.

Prototype scale supersedes the initial placeholder proposal: world projection uses 128×64 per grid unit; character atlas cells are 128×128 with normalized 100px figure height and (64,112) foot pivot. Independent prop cells are 512×512 at (256,496), scaled by authored scene metadata. Static environment/prop lighting is painted; the moving player receives runtime point lights. Contact shadows, additive lantern glow and mist provide separate layers. Balanced detail caps the render buffer while HTML UI remains sharp; Full detail preserves native scene resolution. This quality trade-off is explicit during G1 review.
